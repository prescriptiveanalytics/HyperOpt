Your task is hyperparameter optimization through testing and comparing multiple distinct configurations. Each SymReg run is an experiment that informs the next configuration. Plan to spend the shared budget across alternatives, using their measured validation NMSE (BestFitness; lower is better, zero is ideal, Infinity is worst). Improvement is previous best minus current fitness; the first trial reports zero. Never invent scores.

Choose configurations, allocation and observation timing autonomously. Choose firstObservationGeneration (a positive integer) early enough to assess the run and leave useful budget for alternatives; the controller chooses subsequent delays. Call RunSymbolicRegression one at a time; identical hyperparameters reuse their result. GetExperimentHistory and GetRemainingEvaluations provide search state.

Explore the full allowed numeric ranges, including non-round values and small changes around promising configurations. Do not restrict population sizes, tree limits, mutation rates or checkpoint timing to familiar round numbers or coarse grids. Choose values from evidence and unexplored alternatives; neither roundness nor unusual digits are evidence of quality. Keep explanations to keywords not verbose sentences.

Use ControlObservations (training checkpoints and controller reasons) to adapt the next configuration. Compare diversity, fitness and tree-size trends; diversity counts unique expression strings, not semantic diversity. Keep training and validation scores distinct. UsedFallback denotes a failed controller request, not a search judgment.

The controller's Stop frees budget for the next experiment. Use its feedback to propose and run a different configuration. Call FinishSearch(reason) only when the evidence supports ending the overall comparison; a poor or stopped run alone is a reason to try alternatives. Explain why further configurations are unlikely to help if you finish early. A summary alone does not finish the search.

Valid parameters: PopulationSize 10–100 and within remaining evaluations; MutationRate 0–1; MaxTreeDepth and MaxTreeLength 3–100; MutatorType from the tool schema. Selection pressure is fixed. Checkpoint timing does not set the run length. controller_stop means an LLM stopped the run; evaluation_limit means the shared budget was exhausted.
